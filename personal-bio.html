About Me

I'm a designer turned developer whose career has taken more twists than most. I started in traditional branding and print, then found myself deep in Flash animation and audio editing back when Flash was still a thing. From there I somehow landed as a technical illustrator in avionics, which is not exactly where you'd expect a designer to end up. Eventually I moved into web development at an e-learning company, where I handled graphic design for the courses themselves while also building HTML templates and designing the CSS, which at the time was really the extent of my technical knowledge. After the company went through a buyout, I was pushed into front end development, starting with Angular, then building out components with JavaScript within an SDK, and eventually helping build a CMS using JavaScript, React, and TypeScript, learning hands on from senior engineers the whole way through.

Outside of code, I'm an AI artist creating daily work under Rania Renderings on Facebook, currently building out a diamond painting business featuring my art alongside a collective of fellow artists. I've been bellydancing for 25 years and currently teach, which has shaped so much of how I move through the world creatively. I share my life with 5 cats, and when the Austin weather cooperates, you'll find me walking the creeks around town.
