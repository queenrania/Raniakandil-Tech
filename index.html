I started as a technical illustrator, drawing exploded views of aircraft parts nobody outside an engineering room would ever see. Then motion graphics. Then front-end code. Then running my own studio, Thisizit Design, since 2000, no agency, no team, just me and whoever I was building for that week.

Somewhere in twenty-two years of branding, code, video, and design, I stopped thinking of myself as one thing. I'm based in Austin, Texas, and I still get the same rush from solving a hard visual problem that I did the first time it happened. If you've got something that needs to be built right, I'd love to hear about it.
